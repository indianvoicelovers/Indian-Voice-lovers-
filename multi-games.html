Privacy Policy — Multi Games

Last updated: 10 October 2026

App name: Multi Games

Package name: com.ivl.multigames

Games inside the app: Count 99, Cricket Pro, Risk and Win

Developer: Lakky

Contact: Indianvoicelover11@gmail.com

1. Summary

Multi Games is a fully offline game collection. We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. The app does not ask for any permission and does not connect to the internet.

2. Information we do NOT collect

The app does not collect or ask for:

  • your name, e-mail address, phone number or any account details (there is no sign-up or login)
  • your location
  • your contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone
  • device identifiers, advertising ID or usage analytics
  • crash reports sent to any server

The app contains no advertisements, no analytics tools and no third-party tracking libraries.

3. Information stored on your device

To remember your choices, the games save a few small settings only on your own device. This data never leaves your phone.

GameWhat is saved
Count 99Language choice, dark mode on/off, voice announcements on/off
Risk and WinTarget score, number of players, maximum rolls per turn
Cricket ProNothing is saved

Player names that you type in Risk and Win are used only while the game is running and are not saved.

You can delete all of this at any time by clearing the app's storage in your phone settings or by uninstalling the app.

4. Text-to-speech (voice announcements)

The games read out game events (for example "Computer chose 7" or "Six runs") using the text-to-speech engine already installed on your device (such as Google Text-to-Speech or Samsung TTS). Only the game's own messages are spoken. The app itself does not send any text anywhere. How the speech engine works is governed by that engine provider's own privacy policy.

5. Permissions

Multi Games does not request any Android permissions.

6. Third parties

We do not share data with third parties, because we do not collect any. The only outside component the app uses is your device's built-in text-to-speech engine, as described above.

7. Children

The app is suitable for a general audience. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it also collects none from children.

8. Security

Since no data is sent off your device, there is no personal data on our side to be exposed. Your saved settings stay in the app's private storage on your phone.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if a future version adds internet features), this policy will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change.

10. Contact us

If you have any question about this policy, write to: Indianvoicelover11@gmail.com

ऐप में कोई विज्ञापन, कोई एनालिटिक्स टूल और कोई थर्ड-पार्टी ट्रैकिंग लाइब्रेरी नहीं है।

गेम अपनी घटनाएँ (जैसे "Computer chose 7" या "Six runs") आपके फ़ोन में पहले से लगे टेक्स्ट-टू-स्पीच इंजन (जैसे Google Text-to-Speech या Samsung TTS) से बोलते हैं। सिर्फ़ गेम के अपने संदेश बोले जाते हैं। ऐप खुद कोई टेक्स्ट कहीं नहीं भेजता। उस स्पीच इंजन का काम उसी इंजन की कंपनी की अपनी प्राइवेसी पॉलिसी के हिसाब से चलता है।
