Privacy Policy — Multi Games

Last updated: 28 October 2026

App name: Multi Games

Package name: com.ivl.multigames

Games inside the app: Count 99, Cricket Pro, Risk and Win, Music Quiz

Developer: Lakky

Contact: Indianvoicelover11@gmail.com

1. Summary

Multi Games is a collection of games that you play after signing in with an account. The app needs an internet connection for sign-in and online play. We do not sell your personal information, and the app has no advertisements, no analytics tools and no third-party tracking libraries.

This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, who processes it for us, how long we keep it, and how you can ask us to delete it.

4. Permissions

The only Android permission the app uses is Internet access, needed for sign-in and online play. The app does not ask for any other permission.

5. Sign-in with Google

If you choose “Continue with Google”, sign-in opens in your phone's web browser and Google handles the sign-in. We receive only the basic account details needed to create your account (such as your e-mail address; Google may also send your name and profile picture address, which the app does not show to other players). Google's own privacy policy applies to what Google does with your data.

6. Text-to-speech (voice announcements)

The games read out game events (for example “Computer chose 7” or “Six runs”) using the text-to-speech engine already installed on your device (such as Google Text-to-Speech or Samsung TTS). The app does not send this text anywhere. How the speech engine works is governed by that engine provider's own privacy policy.

7. Service providers who process data for us

We do not sell data or share it for advertising. We use the following services to run the app:

  • Supabase — database and sign-in service that stores accounts, rooms, game data and chat (our project is hosted in the Singapore region).
  • Google — only if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Brevo — sends account e-mails, such as e-mail confirmation and password reset.
  • GitHub — hosts this policy page and the password-reset page, and holds a private, access-restricted backup copy of our database that is refreshed regularly. The developer may also save an export copy on his own private device. Backup copies are used only to restore the service or to deal with abuse and security problems, and they are deleted automatically after 30 days.

Data is sent over secure (HTTPS) connections. Our service providers may also process technical data, such as your IP address, in their server logs. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to deal with serious abuse.

8. How long we keep data

  • Account information is kept while your account exists.
  • Chat messages are deleted automatically after 14 days. Abuse reports are kept only as long as needed to review them (see the 30-day report cases in section 9).
  • If we ban an account for abuse, we keep a one-way scrambled (hashed) form of its e-mail address, which cannot be turned back into the address, so that the ban cannot be avoided by signing up again. It is kept until we lift the ban.
  • When you delete your account (see section 9), your account data is deleted at once. A limited safety copy is kept for 7 days, abuse report cases are kept for 30 days, and copies of deleted data may remain in our backups for up to 30 days, after which they are deleted automatically.

9. Account deletion — available

Account deletion is available. You can delete your account and the data linked to it at any time, in the app or from this page.

Option A — inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open Multi Games and sign in.
  2. On the home screen, open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Read the warning and tap Yes, delete. Your account is deleted and you are logged out.

Option B — without the app (for example, if you uninstalled it)

  1. Send an e-mail to Indianvoicelover11@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Multi Games account”.
  2. Write from the e-mail address you use to sign in to the app (or tell us that address), so we can find your account.
  3. We will delete your account, normally within 30 days. The 7-day and 30-day safety copies described below still apply.

What is deleted, and what may remain

  • Deleted at once: your account (e-mail address and sign-in), your profile including the optional mobile number, the rooms you created, your game data and the chat messages you sent.
  • Kept for 7 days as a safety copy: your account ID, player name, e-mail address, the dates of sign-up and deletion, and copies of abuse reports, blocks and chat messages linked to your account. We use it only to review abuse and stop people who sign up just to misuse the service, and then it is deleted automatically.
  • Kept for 30 days — abuse report cases: if a player is reported, we keep a copy of that report (the reason, the e-mail addresses of the players involved, the room details and the last chat messages of that room) for 30 days, even if one of the accounts is deleted in the meantime. We use it only to review the report, and then it is deleted automatically.
  • Copies in our daily backup, which are deleted automatically after 30 days.

You can also log out at any time from Settings > Logout, and clear data stored on your phone as described in section 3.

10. Your choices

  • Giving a mobile number is optional — you can skip it.
  • Chat works only in rooms where the room creator turned it on, and the app shows chat rules first.
  • You can report or block a player, and a room creator can remove a player.
  • You can delete your account at any time, in the app under Settings > Delete account (section 9).

11. Children

Multi Games is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Online rooms can include chat with other players. If you believe a child has given us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

12. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and access to the database is limited by access rules so that players can only read and change what they are allowed to. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

13. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this policy and change the “Last updated” date above.

14. Contact us

If you have any question about this policy, or want to delete your data, write to: Indianvoicelover11@gmail.com
